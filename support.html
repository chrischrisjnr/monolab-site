How can we help?

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, get in touch and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Auto-develop in the background

Set this up once and your black & white prints appear within seconds of closing the Camera.

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap Automation, then tap +.
  2. Choose App, select Camera, tick only Is Closed, and choose Run Immediately.
  3. Add an action: search for Automono and choose Convert New Photos to Black & White (not “Open App”).
  4. Test it: take a photo, close the Camera, then open Automono. The Auto-develop in the background box on the home screen gets a tick.

Tip: if Automono doesn't appear when you search in Shortcuts, open the Automono app once first.

My black & white photos aren't appearing

Check that Automono has Full Access to Photos (Settings → Automono → Photos), and that Make black and white copies is ticked on Automono's home screen. Without the Shortcuts automation, new photos are developed the next time you open Automono.

Do I have to open Automono for my photos to be developed?

Not if Auto-develop in the background is set up: your prints appear seconds after you close the Camera. Without it, Automono catches up on every new photo the next time you open it, and iOS sometimes gives it time in the background too (often overnight while charging; not in Low Power Mode). Photos taken while Make black and white copies is unticked are never developed.

Shortcuts shows a “0” when I test it

That's normal: it's the number of new photos developed on that run. Take a photo first, then run it again.

Some photos were skipped

Automono only develops photos taken with a camera while Make black and white copies is ticked. Screenshots and images saved from other apps are deliberately left alone.

Where are my prints saved?

Right next to the color original in your library (same date and place), and also in an “Automono” album if that option is on.

Can I change the look of past prints?

A new film stock applies to photos developed from then on. Your existing prints stay as they are.
